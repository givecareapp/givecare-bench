# InvisibleBench 6.0 design note

Type: working design. This is a plan for the next benchmark version, not the
current scoring method or a published validation result. The approved
architecture is Inspect-native and subtractive. The proposed horizon and human
baseline remain research questions until they pass the evidence gates below.

## Aim

Measure harder caregiver AI **Safety** and **Care** in conversations. The
distinctive construct is two-person harm: effects on the caregiver, the person
receiving care, and their relationship. Repeated turns should test whether a
model keeps a sound response under pushback, retraction, fatigue, and a change
of topic. Neither a polished refusal nor a crisis number alone proves good
care.

The benchmark is independent of GiveCare product direction. It describes
model behavior under recorded test conditions. It does not certify a product,
clinical advice, clinician agreement, or caregiver outcomes.

## Three sources of discipline

| Layer | What we take | What we must show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument review, informed by [Epoch's benchmark-review rubric](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/cmt-benchmark/review) | Inspectable tasks, scorer, harness settings, version consistency, and an error audit that looks for impossible tasks, false FAILs, false PASSes, and checks that miss their claimed construct. | A published self-review with defects and score sensitivity disclosed. We may say it uses Epoch's rubric; only Epoch can assign its own **Verified** verdict. |
| Measurement design, informed by [METR's time-horizon method](https://metr.org/time-horizons/) and [limitations note](https://metr.org/notes/2026-01-22-time-horizon-limitations/) | Repeated runs, comparable settings, a human reference on matched cases, uncertainty, and analysis of transcript defects. | Complete coverage and uncertainty for each reported number. A proposed conversation horizon needs valid turn-level judgments and enough varied conversations; it is not a headline metric yet. |
| Caregiving construct, owned here | Two-person risk, multi-turn continuity, separate Safety and Care, legitimate-behavior controls alongside harmful-behavior cases. | Checks whose claimed behavior is actually exercised, with paired controls and narrow pass meanings. |

METR's [golden-baseline review](https://metr.org/notes/2026-03-10-many-swe-bench-passing-prs-would-not-be-merged-into-main/)
motivates a possible blinded comparison against real professional responses to
the same caregiving cases. That is a study design proposal, not a current
baseline. A benchmark judge's agreement with reviewers would be evidence about
the judge; it would not establish clinical validity.

## Rules for a defensible result

- Keep the frozen 5.7 cases and answers as evidence. Re-derive them offline
  before deleting an old execution path. Version every scoring change and
  explain every frozen FAIL that changes to another verdict.
- Use one judge path per check: yes/no probabilities over fixed evidence,
  code-owned cue-then-reply rules, and a frozen uncertainty band. Save each
  request and its attempt before dispatch. A verdict must replay from the plan
  and saved answers without another model call.
- Keep `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNCLEAR`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` distinct. Report exact
  possible outcomes for each UNCLEAR, denominators, and missing coverage.
  Never turn ambiguity into a favorable settled verdict.
- Report Safety and Care separately. Never make a composite score or model
  rank. Saturated checks serve as regression tripwires; they do not earn a
  headline claim merely by being easy to pass.
- For every new headline check, freeze a harmful case and a matched legitimate
  case. Probe approval and spending are separate from the offline control
  design. Do not use development exemplars as independent validation.
- Match model serving policy, budget, prompt, and harness as closely as the
  research question requires. Report differences and incomplete runs. Repeats
  of one scenario do not create distinct scenario coverage.
- Keep human review outside runtime scoring. Freeze an independently reviewed
  set, name reviewers and their relation to the author, and report per-check
  cutoff, precision, recall, UNCLEAR rate, and coverage. Do not infer those
  quantities from AI self-triage or a development set.

## Inspect-native shape (approved architecture)

Extend upstream [Inspect](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/tasks.html), without
forking it:

| Component | InvisibleBench responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `tasks/` | Safety-and-Care evaluation, plus exemplar and paired-control tasks. |
| `solvers/` | Authored multi-turn conversations and recorded branches. |
| `scorers/` | The existing requests-and-rules engine, one judge backend, saved attempts, budget reservations, exact resume, and offline replay. Each `Score` carries check verdicts, possible verdicts, and cited turns. |
| `metrics/` | Separate Safety and Care observations with counts and UNCLEAR ranges. Repeat-run error bars require matched coverage. A conversation-horizon metric is experimental until validated. |
| `scanners/` | Transcript-integrity review for truncation, evaluation awareness, broken scenarios, and canned safety text that games the grader. [Inspect Scout](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/scanners.html) is the integration point; model-backed scans need a scoped cost approval. |
| Jury Card | One citable report per run from Inspect logs: rates and denominators, uncertainty, cited FAILs, settings, provenance, what it measures and misses, self-review status, and human commentary kept outside the verdict ledger. |

The new components replace the old export, scan, example/probe, control-planning,
and bundle-reading paths once each replacement is proven. Historical 5.7 runs
remain frozen and can be replayed from the 5.7 checkout. A fresh 6.0 clone
should run and score through Inspect without the `bench` command.

## Proof order

1. Re-derive the four frozen 5.7 bundles from saved answers, verdict for
   verdict, before removing any old path. Record any difference and its cause.
2. Prove the new path with a fixture judge: a scored Inspect log, offline
   replay without a judge client, a passing repository gate, and a fresh clone
   that needs no legacy CLI. Then remove the duplicate paths.
3. Source-review each owner-confirmed grader defect; repair it with a paired
   exemplar, version bump, offline frozen rescore, and an explanation for every
   FAIL-to-non-FAIL change. Keep uncertain owner calls uncertain.
4. Freeze matched positive and negative controls for each new headline check.
   Run probes only under a separately approved ceiling.
5. Obtain independent human labels on a frozen set with stated coverage.
   Publish per-check calibration and disagreement, including UNCLEARs and
   reviewer relationships. Do not claim clinician validation without it.
6. Generate Jury Cards and method documentation from the Inspect evidence.
   A conversation horizon or professional baseline can become a headline only
   after its task distribution, labels, uncertainty, and limits are defensible.

## Open measurement questions

- **Conversation horizon:** A turn number at which the chance of remaining
  safe crosses a chosen threshold is only meaningful if conversations have
  comparable opportunities to fail. Define how UNCLEAR, early termination,
  varying length, and scenario mix enter the survival curve before fitting it.
  Follow METR's warning that horizon estimates can be imprecise and highly
  sensitive to task selection.
- **Professional baseline:** Professionals would need the same information and
  constraints as models, blinded review of both sets of responses, and a
  protocol for reviewer disagreement. The current review packet does not
  establish this baseline.
- **Transcript integrity:** Scanner findings are validity flags, not automatic
  verdict edits. Define whether a flagged run is excluded or annotated before
  looking at model results. Do not run paid scanners without approval.
- **External review:** An Epoch-style self-review is not an Epoch verdict.
  Independent reviewers and public change control are separate later steps.

This note records intent and proof obligations. The current released method
remains in [Method](methodology.md) and [Evaluator validation](validation.md).
